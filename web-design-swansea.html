What a website build looks like

If you are a Swansea business thinking about a new website or a replacement for an old one, here is what the process looks like from start to finish:

  1. First, a conversation. We find out about your business, your customers, what you want people to do when they find your site, and what you have already (if anything). This is a phone call or video call and usually takes about twenty minutes. No commitment required.
  2. We plan what the site needs. Most Swansea small business websites need a home page, a services or products page, an about page, and a contact page. Depending on your business, there might be a few more. We agree the structure and talk through the content before we start building anything.
  3. We build it. Once we have the content and any photos or images you want to use, we get on with building. This stage typically takes three to five days for a standard small business site. We do not outsource and we do not use page-builder tools — everything is built properly.
  4. You review and we refine. We send you a preview link. You tell us what you want changed. One to two rounds of feedback is normal and we turn changes around quickly.
  5. We go live together. We handle the technical setup — hosting, domain, SSL, email configuration — and your site goes live. From first conversation to live website, most Swansea clients are up and running within one to two weeks.
