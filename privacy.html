Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 May 2026

This privacy policy explains how TimTom Studio handles personal information through our website, services and apps. TimTom Studio is based near Merthyr Tydfil in South Wales, United Kingdom.

Information We Receive

If you contact us by email, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, business details and project information. We use this information to reply to your enquiry and provide requested services.

Website Analytics and Cookies

Our website uses essential storage to remember cookie choices. We do not currently use advertising cookies. If Google Analytics is added later, analytics will only run where a visitor has allowed analytics cookies through the cookie banner.

You can change your cookie choice at any time from the cookie settings link.

Apps Developed by TimTom Studio

Counta

Counta is currently in development. The app is designed so countdowns, goals, savings targets and reminders are stored locally on your device unless a future version clearly explains otherwise.

Counta may use local notifications if you choose to enable reminders. Local notifications are generated by your device.

Client Projects

If we build a website, booking system or app for a client, privacy requirements may vary depending on the project. We will discuss data handling, third-party services and privacy notices with each client as part of the project.

Sharing Information

We do not sell personal information. We may use trusted providers where needed to deliver our services, such as hosting, email or app platform services.

Data Retention

We keep enquiry and project information only for as long as reasonably needed for communication, service delivery, record keeping and legal obligations.

Your Rights

You can contact us to ask about personal information we may hold about you, or to request that it is corrected or deleted where applicable.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact TimTom Studio at hello@timtomstudio.co.uk.
